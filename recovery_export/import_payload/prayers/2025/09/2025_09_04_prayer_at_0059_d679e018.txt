My beautiful friend is someone who has such a kind heart and lives life through the lens of only being of service to others. Throughout her life she has struggled quite often with being taken advantage of as well as many other negative situations. However heartbreaking these events are she has a preserving spirit of compassion. I pray that someday she will be able to find happiness and healing in life organically, and all the good she has done to others will be done unto her.

Generated Prayer:
Gracious Father, You who are the defender of the vulnerable and rewarder of the faithful, we lift up our precious sister who has poured out her life in service to others. As You promised that those who sow in tears shall reap in joy (Psalm 126:5), we ask that You would restore and multiply every kindness she has shown. Guard her tender heart, Lord, teaching her to balance compassion with wisdom, and surround her with people who will cherish and protect her spirit rather than exploit her generosity. We pray that You would bring deep healing to the wounds of her past and lead her into the abundant life that Jesus promised, where she can experience the pure joy of giving while also receiving the love and care she so richly deserves. Amen.
