Gracious Father, You who created time itself and called us to be faithful stewards of every moment, we lift our entire community to You. As Your Word reminds us that whatever we do should be done with all our heart, as working for You and not for men (Colossians 3:23), we ask that You ignite in us an all-consuming passion for Your presence. Fill us with such a deep love for You and our neighbors that any moment spent in idleness (except for healthy rest and keeping the Sabbath) becomes uncomfortable to our spirit. Transform our desires, Lord, until acts of service and love flow as naturally as breath, and grant us the discipline to steward our time for Your glory. May our lives become a living testimony of productive love, bringing joy to Your heart and blessing to those around us. Amen.
