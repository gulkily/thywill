Loving Father, You promise to be with us always, even to the end of the age. Guide us as we seek to deepen their awareness of Your constant presence. May we, like David who wrote "I have set the Lord always before me" (Psalm 16:8), develop a heightened consciousness of Your nearness in every moment. Help us to recognize Your gentle whispers throughout our day, transforming ordinary moments into sacred encounters with You. Grant us the grace to practice stillness and attentiveness, knowing that You are indeed closer than our very breath. Amen.
