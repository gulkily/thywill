A job for brother Usman

Generated Prayer:
Gracious Father, provider of all good things, we lift up our brother Usman in his search for employment. As You promised to supply all our needs according to Your riches in glory (Philippians 4:19), we ask that You would open doors of opportunity for him and guide his steps to the right workplace. Grant him wisdom in his job search, peace during this season of waiting, and the position that will best use the gifts and talents You have given him. May this employment bring honor to Your name and allow him to be a light in his workplace. Amen.
