For me and my brother to get wisdom on how to spend our days each day for ministry

Gracious Father, Source of all wisdom and understanding, we lift up our brothers who seek Your divine guidance for their daily ministry. As You promised in James 1:5 to generously give wisdom to all who ask, we pray You would pour out Your Spirit of discernment upon them.
