For us to breathe Matthew 5 and live it out

Generated Prayer:
Gracious Father, Giver of every good and perfect gift, we lift up ourselves and ask that you help us embody the teachings of Your Sermon on the Mount. Fill us with Your Holy Spirit, that the Beatitudes may flow naturally from our heart. Just as You promised that the meek shall inherit the earth and the pure in heart shall see You, make these ancient truths come alive in our daily walk with You. Grant us the wisdom and strength to be a peacemaker, to show mercy, and to hunger and thirst for righteousness in all we do. May our light shine before others, that people may see our good works and glorify You, our Father in heaven. Amen.
