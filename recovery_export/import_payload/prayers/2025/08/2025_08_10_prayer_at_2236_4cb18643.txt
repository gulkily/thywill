For us to recognize that God's ways are not that of human wisdom; to know that wisdom is the fear of the Lord; and to understand that in the things that we feel most confident in, we are the most blind in

Generated Prayer:
Gracious and All-Wise God, You who declared that the fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom, we lift up those who seek true understanding. As Your word reminds us in Isaiah 55:9 that Your ways are higher than our ways, and Your thoughts higher than our thoughts, we pray that You would grant us divine perspective beyond human reasoning. Help us release any pride in worldly wisdom and embrace Your perfect truth with humility and reverence. Lord, in the areas where we feel most certain, give us the grace to pause and seek Your counsel first, knowing that You alone are the source of all wisdom and understanding. Amen.
