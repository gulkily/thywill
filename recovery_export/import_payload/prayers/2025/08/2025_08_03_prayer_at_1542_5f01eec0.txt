For the bar advocates of Massachusetts to get paid enough

Generated Prayer:
Gracious and Just Lord, who established laws and justice through Your servant Moses, we lift up the bar advocates of Massachusetts who serve as voices for those in need. As You promise in Proverbs 31:9 to "defend the rights of the poor and needy," we ask You to ensure these dedicated attorneys receive fair compensation for their important work. Grant wisdom to those in authority to recognize and properly value the essential service these advocates provide to our justice system. May You open the way for appropriate funding and resources, so these servants of justice can continue their vital mission of ensuring equal access to legal representation for all. Amen.
