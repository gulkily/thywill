For us to be nice to people to love them unconditionally rather than use social tactic to have them be nice back to us

Generated Prayer:
Gracious Father, source of perfect love, we lift up those who desire to grow in genuine compassion. Help them cultivate a heart that love others purely, without seeking anything in return, just as You have loved us unconditionally (1 John 4:19). Transform their relationships from ones based on social strategy into authentic connections grounded in Your divine love. Grant them the strength to show kindness even when it isn't reciprocated, and fill their spirit with the same sacrificial love that Christ demonstrated for us. Amen.
