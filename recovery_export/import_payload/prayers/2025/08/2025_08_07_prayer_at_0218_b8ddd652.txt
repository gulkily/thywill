For rest and courage for Raymond

Generated Prayer:
Gracious Father, our loving God who gives rest to the weary and strength to the weak, we lift up our brother Raymond to You today. Just as You promised rest for all who come to You with heavy burdens, grant Raymond peaceful rest for his body, mind, and spirit. Fill him with the same courage You gave to Joshua when You commanded him to be strong and courageous, knowing You would be with him wherever he went. Lord, renew his strength so that he may soar on wings like eagles, run and not grow weary, walk and not faint, as You promised in Isaiah 40:31. In Your merciful name we pray. Amen.
