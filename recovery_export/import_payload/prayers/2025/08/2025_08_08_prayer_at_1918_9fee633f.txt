For us to know that God is watching, proctoring, and gazing on us - and for that to raise the awe, reverance, and fear of God in our actions

Generated Prayer:
Gracious and Holy Father, who sees all things and from whom nothing is hidden, we lift up ourselves before You. As You reminded Your servant David that Your eyes are always upon us (Psalm 139:1-4), we ask that You would heighten our awareness of Your constant, loving presence. Help us to walk in reverent awe of Your majesty with the deep respect that transforms daily choices and actions. May we, like Isaiah, catch a glimpse of Your holiness that stuns us and forever changes how we live and move in this world. Let Your Spirit remind us moment by moment that we stand on holy ground in Your presence. Amen.
