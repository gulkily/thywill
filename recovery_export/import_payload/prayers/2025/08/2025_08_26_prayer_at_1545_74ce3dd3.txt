For us to be able to love God and our neighbors and to prioritize those two commandments in our every action (Use "us" instead of "them" when making prayer)

Gracious Father, Source of all love and compassion, we come before You acknowledging our need for Your transforming power in our lives. Help us to embrace Your first and greatest commandment - to love You with all our heart, soul, mind and strength. Shape our thoughts, words, and deeds so that everything we do flows from our love for You and extends to our neighbors. As You have promised in Ezekiel 36:26 to give Your people a new heart, create in us clean hearts that naturally overflow with Your divine love. Grant us wisdom to evaluate our daily choices through the lens of these two commandments, and give us courage to adjust our priorities when they do not align with Your perfect will. Amen.
