For deceptive people to become honest, and for people to be kind to them

Generated Prayer:
Holy Father, Source of all Truth and Mercy, we lift up those who struggle with deception and dishonesty. As You transformed the heart of Zacchaeus from deceit to righteousness, we ask You to work powerfully in their lives. Grant them the courage to embrace truth, knowing that Your perfect love casts out all fear. For those around them, fill their hearts with grace and understanding, remembering that we all fall short of Your glory. May Your Spirit guide these precious souls toward integrity, and help us show them the same patience and kindness that You have shown to us. Through Your unfailing love, as promised in Ephesians 4:15, help them speak truth in love. Amen.
