Clarity, decisive spirit for Iban

Generated Prayer:
Gracious Father, source of all wisdom and light, we lift up our brother Iban before Your throne. As You promised to give wisdom generously to all who ask (James 1:5), we pray for clear direction and discernment to fill his mind and heart. Lord, remove any clouds of confusion or doubt that may be hindering his path, and grant him the decisive spirit that comes from Your perfect peace. Guide his steps with Your unfailing light, just as You led Your people with the pillar of fire through the wilderness. Amen.
