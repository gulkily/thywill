For Ben's work to flourish and produce fruit

Generated Prayer:
Gracious Father, provider of all good things, we lift up our brother Ben and the work of his hands. Just as You blessed the work of Joseph in Egypt and Daniel in Babylon, we ask that You would prosper Ben's efforts and grant him wisdom to steward his responsibilities well. May the fruit of his labor bring glory to Your name and benefit to those around him, as You promised that those who trust in You will be like trees planted by streams of water, bearing fruit in season (Psalm 1:3). Guide his decisions, multiply his impact, and let him see the eternal significance of his daily work. Amen.
