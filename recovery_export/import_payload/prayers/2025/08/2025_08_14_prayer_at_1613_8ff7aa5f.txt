For a brother in Christ's vocation and his ability to love his wife

Generated Prayer:
Gracious Father, Author of love and Giver of purpose, We lift up our brother before You, asking that You would guide his steps in his calling and strengthen his marriage. As You have promised to direct the paths of those who trust in You (Proverbs 3:5-6), make clear the way he should go in his vocation. Fill his heart with the same sacrificial love that Christ showed for His church, enabling him to cherish and honor his wife in ways that reflect Your perfect love. Grant them both wisdom, patience, and understanding as they grow together in Your grace. In Jesus' name, Amen.
