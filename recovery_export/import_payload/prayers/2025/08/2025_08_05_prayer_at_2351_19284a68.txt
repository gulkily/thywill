For a Christian brother to escape all mentality of Christian legalism and pride and to feel comfort and motivation in acknowledging and receiving the grace of Jesus

Generated Prayer:
Loving Father, who sent Your Son to set us free from the burden of legalism, we lift up our brother who struggles with pride and religious perfectionism. Just as You promised in Matthew 11:30 that Your yoke is easy and Your burden is light, help them experience the liberating truth that we are saved by grace through faith, not by works. Release them from the chains of self-righteousness and fill their heart with the deep comfort of Your unconditional love. May Your Holy Spirit guide them into a fresh revelation of Jesus' finished work on the cross, bringing newfound joy and freedom in their walk with You. Amen.
