May all of us find respect and understanding for each other

Generated Prayer:
Gracious and loving Father, You created every person in Your image and commanded us to love one another as You have loved us. We lift up our community and ask that You pour out Your spirit of unity and understanding among us. Help each person to see the divine worth in others, just as Jesus demonstrated when He washed His disciples' feet. Guide us to follow Your word from Philippians 2:3, considering others as more significant than ourselves. May Your peace reign in our hearts and Your wisdom guide our words and actions as we interact with one another. Amen.
