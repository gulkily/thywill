Heal the pain in the world

Generated Prayer:
Loving Father, Source of all comfort and peace, we lift up to You the deep pain and suffering that exists in our world today. As You promised to heal the brokenhearted and bind up their wounds (Psalm 147:3), we ask that You pour out Your healing balm upon those who are hurting. Grant wisdom to leaders, compassion to caregivers, and strength to those working for peace and reconciliation. May Your Spirit move across our troubled earth, bringing hope to the hopeless, rest to the weary, and restoration to broken places. In Your mercy, make us instruments of Your peace and healing. Amen.
