Gracious Father, source of all wisdom and truth, we lift up our brother/sister who seeks Your divine discernment. Grant them Your perfect balance of righteousness and mercy, as You have shown us through Christ Jesus who was "full of grace and truth" (John 1:14). Help them distinguish between godly conviction and human condemnation, giving them eyes to see sin as You see it - with both justice and redemptive love. Lord, protect their heart from deception while filling them with Your Spirit of wisdom, that they may respond to wrongdoing with Your perfect blend of holiness and compassion. Guide them to that sacred place where truth and love meet, just as Your Word declares that we should "speak the truth in love" (Ephesians 4:15). Amen.
