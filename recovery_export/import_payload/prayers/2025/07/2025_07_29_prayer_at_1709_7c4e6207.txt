For people with mental health struggles to praies God and give thanks to him when things are hard

Generated Prayer:
Loving Father, we lift up our brothers and sisters who face mental health challenges. As You promised to be near to the brokenhearted and save the crushed in spirit (Psalm 34:18), draw close to them now. Grant them moments of clarity to see Your goodness even in difficult seasons, and give them strength to offer praise when their hearts feel heavy. Help them remember that You are the God who turns mourning into dancing, and that Your faithfulness endures through every storm. May Your Spirit comfort them and remind them that nothing can separate them from Your love, just as You assured us through Paul in Romans 8:38-39. Amen.
