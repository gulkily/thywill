For James’ friends and family to turn to Christ

Generated Prayer:
Loving Father, You promise in Your Word that You desire all people to come to knowledge of the truth and be saved. We lift up the friends and family of James to You today, asking that You would open their hearts to receive Your Son Jesus Christ. Just as You drew Lydia's heart to respond to Paul's message in Acts 16, draw these precious souls to Yourself. Grant them eyes to see Your glory, ears to hear Your truth, and hearts soft to Your gentle calling. May Your Holy Spirit work powerfully in their lives, bringing them into Your family and filling James with joy as he witnesses their transformation. Amen.
