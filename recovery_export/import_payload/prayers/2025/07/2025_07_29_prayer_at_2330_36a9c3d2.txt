For our brother Rainier’s family’s travels

Generated Prayer:
Gracious Father, who led Your people through the wilderness and guided the wise men by Your star, we lift up our brother Rainier's family as they journey. Watch over them as they travel, surround them with Your protective angels, and grant them safe passage to their destination and back home again. Just as You promised to be with Joshua wherever he went (Joshua 1:9), be present with this family, keeping them secure in Your care and blessing their time together. Through Christ our Lord, Amen.
