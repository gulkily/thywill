For the victims of the plane crash in Bangladesh and their families

Generated Prayer:
Merciful Father, who comforts those who mourn and heals the brokenhearted, we lift up the victims of the Bangladesh plane crash and their grieving families. Grant peace and strength to those who have lost loved ones, and surround them with Your tender care in this time of profound sorrow. As You promised in Psalm 34:18 to be near the brokenhearted, draw especially close to these families, guiding them through the valley of shadows with Your unfailing love. Lord, provide wisdom and support to the rescue workers, medical teams, and all those helping in the aftermath of this tragedy. Amen.
