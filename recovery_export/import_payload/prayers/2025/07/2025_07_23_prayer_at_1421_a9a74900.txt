For us to worship God, not pleasure

Generated Prayer:
Gracious Father in Heaven, we lift up our friend who desires to put You first in their life. Help them to find their deepest satisfaction in Your presence, remembering Your promise that in Your presence is fullness of joy (Psalm 16:11). Transform their heart's desires until they can truly say like David that You alone are their portion and their cup. Give them wisdom to recognize when they are making idols of earthly pleasures, and grant them the strength to surrender those things at Your feet. May they experience the truth that You are most glorified when they are most satisfied in You. Amen.
