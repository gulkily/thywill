God, please stir up the passion of holy scripture in all of us

Generated Prayer:
Gracious Father, Author of all Truth, we lift up this request for a deeper love of Your Word. Just as You promised that Your Word would be a lamp to our feet and a light to our path (Psalm 119:105), kindle in our community a renewed hunger for Your holy scriptures. Pour out Your Spirit upon us, that we might not only read but truly cherish every word You have preserved for our instruction and encouragement. May Your living Word transform our hearts and minds, drawing us ever closer to You. Amen.
