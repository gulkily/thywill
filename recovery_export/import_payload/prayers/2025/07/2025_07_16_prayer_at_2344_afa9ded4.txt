I pray that we, when performing work or any task, do not forget about God as we lock in on the task - for such a state of being makes us vulnerable to the evil one, and we must meditate and remember God's laws at all times.

Generated Prayer:
Gracious Father, Keeper of our hearts and minds, Help us to remain mindful of Your presence even in the midst of our daily tasks and responsibilities. As Your Word reminds us to "pray without ceasing" (1 Thessalonians 5:17), grant us the spiritual discipline to weave worship and meditation on Your laws throughout our work. Shield us from the enemy's attempts to use busyness as a distraction from Your truth. May we experience the blessing of working as unto You, finding joy in keeping You at the center of all their endeavors. In Jesus' name, Amen.
